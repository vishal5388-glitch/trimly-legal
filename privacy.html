Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Applies to the Trimly app for iPhone, its widgets and its Siri shortcuts

The short version

Trimly collects no data. The subscriptions you add are stored on your iPhone and never sent to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Trimly never asks for your bank login.

Who we are

Trimly is made by Vishal Verma ("we", "us"). You can reach us at svtechllpsupport@gmail.com.

What Trimly stores, and where

Everything you enter (names, prices, billing dates, notes, the label of the card you pay with, how often you used a service, and your ratings) is saved in Trimly's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS file encryption. A short summary (your next renewals and monthly total) is shared with Trimly's own widgets on the same iPhone so they can display it. Nothing is uploaded.

Never enter a card number into Trimly. The "Paid with" field is meant for a label such as "Amex" or "Apple Card".

What Trimly can access, and why

FeatureWhat happens
Scan a screenshotYou pick screenshots with Apple's photo picker. Trimly can see only the images you pick, not the rest of your library. It reads their text on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition, suggests the subscriptions it found, and saves only the ones you confirm. The images and their text are then discarded.
NotificationsOptional reminders before renewals and free trials end, and a monthly check-in. They are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent from a server.
Face ID or passcodeOptional app lock. iOS performs the check. Trimly never receives your face data or passcode.
Siri and ShortcutsIf you ask "What renews this week?", Trimly answers from the data on your iPhone.
SharingThe Wrapped card, CSV export and backups are created on your iPhone and only leave it if you choose to share or save them, through the iOS share sheet.
Cancel linksLinks to a service's account or cancellation page open in Safari. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive or store any of your data. We have no servers.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking software of any kind.
  • We do not ask for bank, card or email logins.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Purchases

Trimly Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment and billing; we never see your payment details. The app learns only, through Apple's StoreKit on your device, whether Pro is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy. Manage or cancel in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

Backups and deleting your data

If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include Trimly's data in that backup under your own Apple settings. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to export or erase on our side.

Children

Trimly is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
