Common questions

How do I import my subscriptions?

Tap Scan a screenshot. The quickest source is your iPhone's own list: open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, take a screenshot, then pick it in Trimly. You can also use screenshots of recent transactions in your bank or card app, or of receipt emails.

The scan missed something or found the wrong price.

Untick anything that isn't a subscription before adding, and tap any price to change it. You can edit every detail later from the subscription's page. Clear, uncropped screenshots work best.

I'm not getting reminders.

Open Trimly > Settings and tap Turn on notifications, or allow them in iPhone Settings > Notifications > Trimly. You can choose the reminder time in Trimly's Settings.

Does Trimly cancel subscriptions for me?

No. Trimly takes you straight to the right page and walks you through it, but only you can cancel with the company or with Apple. When you're done, tap I've cancelled it and Trimly tracks your savings.

The widget says "Open Trimly".

Open the app once after installing or updating. The widget refreshes from the app's data on your iPhone.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Restoring an iPhone backup brings Trimly's data with it. You can also use Settings > Back up to Files in Trimly, then Restore a backup on the new iPhone.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel Trimly Pro?

To restore, open Trimly > Settings > Restore purchases. To cancel, open iPhone Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Trimly Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Does Trimly collect my data?

No. Your subscriptions stay on your iPhone, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.
